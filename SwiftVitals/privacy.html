SwiftVitals Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use SwiftVitals.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or usage data.

Data Processed on Your Mac

When you select a project folder or Xcode result bundle, SwiftVitals receives read-only access to that item. Swift source, local Git history, and test-coverage results are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us.

Local Storage

SwiftVitals stores app settings and security-scoped bookmarks for folders you approve using macOS local storage. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

Network Access and Third Parties

SwiftVitals does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party data-collection SDKs. Its optional MCP server communicates locally over standard input and output with a client you configure.
